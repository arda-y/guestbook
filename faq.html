faq

limits and data policy.

What are the limits?

Message limits are displayed on screen. You can like an entry once per week, and send 2 entries per day.

What data does guestbook collect?

It collects your IP address to enforce the limits mentioned above. When you send a post or like an entry, the app keeps your IP address for 7 days, and then blanks your IP address.

Your name and message are public. Anyone can read them. Do not post information you want to keep private.

This app does not collect any other data. The colour mode you pick (auto, light or dark) is saved in your own browser. It never leaves your device.

How do I report abuse?

To report an entry, contact me@arda0.net.
