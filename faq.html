FAQ

How long is my entry kept?

guestbook entries are not deleted automatically. Your entry stays until an admin removes it.

What are the limits?

Your name can be at most 100 characters. Your message can be at most 500 characters. The form shows how many characters you have left as you type.

Per IP address, guestbook also limits how often you can act: 2 new entries per day, 3 stars per day, and 15 reads per minute. If you go over a limit, guestbook rejects the request with an error. Wait for the limit to reset, then try again.

What data does guestbook collect?

guestbook uses your IP address only to enforce the limits above. guestbook does not require an account. guestbook does not collect your email. guestbook does not link your IP address to a profile.

Your name and message are public. Anyone can read them. Do not post information you want to keep private.

How do I report abuse?

To report an entry, contact me@arda0.net.
